How to connect a Google Sheet
  1. Open the ready-made sheet and press Make a copy.
  2. In your copy, choose Extensions → Apps Script. In the toolbar pick setup and press Run.
  3. Google asks for permission and says the app isn’t verified, because it’s your own copy. Choose Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) and Allow.
  4. Choose Deploy → New deployment, click the gear and pick Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy.
  5. Copy the Web app URL and paste it above. That’s it: your copy is yours alone from then on.
  1. Open your Google Sheet (a new blank one is fine: sheets.new) and choose Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete everything in the editor, paste the script, and press Ctrl + S.
  3. In the toolbar pick setup and press Run. Google says the app isn’t verified, because you made it yourself: choose Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) and Allow.
  4. Choose Deploy → New deployment, click the gear and pick Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy.
  5. Copy the Web app URL and paste it above.

Already deployed an older script? Paste the new one, then use Deploy → Manage deployments → ✎ Edit → Version: New version → Deploy. The link stays the same. Your old rows stay where they are; the app copies everything into a new tab called Hours.

Give friends a ready-made sheet
  1. Make a new blank sheet (sheets.new) and name it Hour Tracker. Keep it empty.
  2. Choose Extensions → Apps Script, delete what’s there, paste this script and press Ctrl + S. It has no secret inside. Don’t run setup or deploy this one.
  3. Choose Share → General access: Anyone with the link · Viewer, copy the link and paste it here:
  4. Build the package with build.py. Friends’ copies of the app then show a “Make a copy” button instead of the script steps.
